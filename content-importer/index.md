# Content Importer

A drop-in "import from your platforms" picker for web apps. Browse what you have saved on Pinterest and YouTube, pick items, review exactly what will be created, and hand the selection to the app you are using, such as a personal wardrobe or recipe collection.

- Read-only access, signed in on each platform's own page
- Nothing is posted, changed or deleted on your accounts
- No media is copied; imports are references to the original content
