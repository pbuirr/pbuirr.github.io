# Mediaprint

A personal music map. It organizes songs by feeling, sound and their relationships, and links out to your music app to listen.

## Data and credits

Tempo and key data from GetSongBPM: <https://getsongbpm.com>

Song identity and relationships from MusicBrainz: <https://musicbrainz.org>

Music suggestions from ListenBrainz: <https://listenbrainz.org>

Mediaprint does not host, stream or download music.
